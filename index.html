The person behind the studio

Built in Belize. Driven by creativity.

I'm Abraham Heavener, founder and creative director of Cielo Studios. I've always been drawn to building things: solving a problem, putting a plan into motion, or bringing an idea to life through film and design.

I started Cielo Studios to carry on my father's work in filmmaking. That same commitment to craft and creativity shapes how we approach every project and every brand we work with.

Cielo means “heaven” in Spanish. For us, it's a name that represents ambition, creativity, and the standard we strive to bring to every client.
